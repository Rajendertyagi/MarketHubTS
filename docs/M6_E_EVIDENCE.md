## Ownership notes

- **M6-B4 rows** (conformance/parity unit work) remain owned by B4. M6-E assembled the real-listener evidence above but does **not** claim B4 acceptance; B4 closeout is a separate gate.
- No row is marked accepted solely because an in-process `handler.fetch` test passed where the master plan requires an actual listener — `tests/mcp/http-integration.test.ts` uses a real `Bun.serve` listener and a pinned official client.
- The previously partial rows C2/C4/C5/C9/C11/C12 are closed by named wire assertions in `tests/mcp/conformance.test.ts` and the STDIO test. **C3 is not fully closed**: its positive error codes are asserted, but the modern-2026-07-28-scoped `-32002`/`-32042` negative assertion is still planned (legacy compatibility is separately covered by `legacy-compat.test.ts`). Ownership is unchanged: these are M6-A/B and M6-A/D rows; M6-E assembled the assertions as evidence, not as a transfer of phase ownership.
- **Documented N/A:** `-32021` (C5) is not triggered by any M6 operation — M6 uses no capability-gated methods (no MRTR/elicitation/sampling/roots), so no request requires a client capability the caller did not declare. This is an explicitly justified non-applicability, not an untested MUST. `-32022` (C3) **is** asserted by the unsupported-version test (HTTP 400, `data.requested`, `data.supported` containing `2026-07-28`) and is no longer N/A.
- **LAN scope changed by user decision** — see [`M6_E_LAN_ACCEPTANCE.md`](M6_E_LAN_ACCEPTANCE.md). The second-device / MacBook / Windows-PC physical LAN requirement was explicitly removed from M6-E. The composed LAN-facing behavior is now covered by the standalone in-process simulation `tests/mcp/lan-simulation.test.ts` (official SDK with mocked `fetch`, in-memory services, real notification bridge, production Host/Origin helper). Physical LAN reachability is **not verified** and is outside the revised M6 scope; the simulation is **not** proof of real LAN connectivity and is not a `NOT TESTED` blocker under the revised scope. The real-listener gate `http-integration.test.ts` remains required and unchanged.
- **SHOULD deviations:** C6's `io.modelcontextprotocol/serverInfo` in result `_meta` is a SHOULD that is **not yet wire-asserted** (planned; owner M6-A/D). It must either be satisfied by a named assertion or recorded here as a reasoned deviation with owner — it cannot be silently waived while claiming "no SHOULD deviations." No other SHOULD deviations are identified in this evidence set. Any future deviation must be recorded here with reason and owner.
