## Real-listener correction evidence (2026-11-24)

Two additional real-`Bun.serve`-listener tests were added to `tests/mcp/http-integration.test.ts` to close M6-E lifecycle/evidence gaps. They are **evidence only** and do not mark M6-D or M6-E accepted.

- **E1 — graceful shutdown with the client left open:** a pinned `2026-07-28` client opens an active `subscriptions/listen` subscription and an in-flight `markethub_health` call; the server calls `handler.close()` **before** closing the client. The subscription's `closed` promise resolves to `"graceful"`, the in-flight call is aborted (not reported as a successful result), and handler/listener/client/bridge/hub/DB/temp-dir cleanup completes deterministically. This supplements (does not replace) the in-process SDK lifecycle tests in `tests/mcp/subscriptions.test.ts`. Supports C12/C17.
- **E2 — bounded SSE through the real listener:** the harness now uses the **same** `wrapMcpHandlerFetch` composition and production caps (`MCP_SSE_MAX_BUFFERED_BYTES`/`MCP_SSE_MAX_BUFFERED_FRAMES`) as `apps/api/src/index.ts`. A deliberately unread `subscriptions/listen` stream opened through the real listener reaches the configured bound, upstream cancellation with `slow_consumer_bound_exceeded` is observed, the downstream terminates with a plain EOF, delivered pre-EOF frames stay within the bound, the already-EOF stream remains closed after further canonical changes, `handler.close()` completes, and canonical publishing is unaffected (no bridge publisher error). **Healthy subscriber continues — IN-PROCESS CONTROL ONLY; the real-listener healthy path is NOT exercised in this overflow scenario** (see the tracked follow-up below). The in-process control reuses the `tests/mcp/slow-consumer.test.ts` pattern and receives every notification; it must **not** be read as full real-listener healthy-path proof. Supports C11/C12 and the slow-consumer mitigation evidence in `M6_D_FANOUT_EVIDENCE.md` §7, with the qualification above.

### Tracked follow-up — Bun wrapped-SSE stall (RESOLVED by Design A; acceptance still gated)

- **Historical symptom (diagnosis only):** a `subscriptions/listen` SSE response produced by the wrapped handler (`apps/api/src/mcp-sse-bound.ts`) and served through a real `Bun.serve()` listener delivered only its first couple of frames and then stalled; the stream did not reach EOF until the handler was closed. The deliberately unread stream in E2 still overflowed and was bounded correctly — the stall affected a **healthy, actively-draining** subscriber on the real listener. The root cause was confirmed in `docs/M6_E_SSE_STALL_DIAGNOSIS.md`: the wrapper's downstream `pull()` returned a pending promise when its buffer was empty, and the drain loop resolved that promise **without calling `controller.enqueue()`**. Per the WHATWG Streams spec, fulfilling a pull promise does not re-invoke `pull()` unless `pullAgain` was set; Bun's single-outstanding-read sink never sets it, so the read hung with the chunk stranded in the private buffer.
- **Resolution (this change):** the wrapper was **replaced** (not patched) with Design A — a standard Web Streams downstream, an application-owned bounded FIFO queue, a synchronous `pull()` demand signal (`pullWanted`), and delivery via `controller.enqueue()` from `pump()`. No waiter promises remain. The full handler shape (`fetch`/`close`/`notify`/`bus`) and the 16 KiB / 96-frame caps and `slow_consumer_bound_exceeded` overflow contract are unchanged. See `docs/M6_E_SSE_STALL_DIAGNOSIS.md` §13 for the implementation record and `docs/M6_D_FANOUT_EVIDENCE.md` §8 for memory accounting.
- **Evidence (real listener, local):** `tests/mcp/sse-design-a.test.ts` › "healthy real-listener subscriber: ack first, >96 notifications in order, no false overflow, correct IDs/filtering" delivers 200/200 notifications through a pinned official client with no false overflow; "slow subscriber (raw TCP saturation)" overflows exactly once with `slow_consumer_bound_exceeded`, app queue high-water 89 frames / 16,287 bytes (within caps), SDK bus listener removed; "slow subscriber reaches plain EOF and receives no frames after overflow" confirms plain EOF and no post-overflow frames; disconnect, multi-client, oversized-frame, pure-Web-Streams-guard, idle-timeout and no-unhandled-rejection tests are in the same file. E2 in `http-integration.test.ts` was updated to use a genuinely saturated raw TCP client (the old 136-frame fetch-no-read workload fit inside Bun's sink buffer and no longer exercised the bound under Design A).
- **Status:** the stall is **fixed and locally verified**. This does **not** mark M6-D or M6-E accepted, does not change the "D slow-consumer residual risk" decision, and does not authorize live provider connection or Stage G. Independent review remains a separate gate.
